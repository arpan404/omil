# Omil

Free, private dictation for Mac. Hold Right Option, speak, and release to type into the app you're using.

Speech recognition and text cleanup run on your Mac. No account or subscription required.

[Download for Mac](https://github.com/arpan404/omil/releases/latest) · [Website](https://omil.arpan.sh)

## Get started

1. Download the DMG, open it, and drag Omil to Applications.
2. Open Omil and follow the setup steps to download the speech models and grant permissions.
3. Place your cursor in a text field, hold Right Option, and start speaking.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 14 or later. Supports English and Nepali on the Mac, and English on iPhone. Initial setup requires Homebrew and an internet connection.

## Features

- **Clean or Verbatim.** Remove filler words and fix grammar, or keep your original wording.
- **Dictionary and snippets.** Add names and terms, or turn a short phrase into reusable text.
- **Dictation history.** Search recent dictations, compare edits, and replay recordings.
- **Flexible controls.** Use a keyboard shortcut, the menu bar, or a floating record button.

You can start a new recording while the previous one is being processed.
